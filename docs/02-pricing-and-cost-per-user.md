# Hakkiri pricing and cost per user

Oct 26, 2026 · Bilaal (owner) with Claude

Launch with a free 10-minute interview, a 3-month Job search plan at £34.99 (30 hours, paid once, the highlighted plan), Monthly at £14.99 for 10 hours a month, and extra hours at 5 for £7.99. Move to £69 and £29 once Hakkiri has reviews and real usage data. A 30-minute practice interview costs about 31p in AI services, about 1p a minute, so a typical user costs £3 to £5 a month. At the launch price a typical user leaves about 60% after VAT, card fees and AI costs. Someone who uses all 10 hours still leaves about 48%, because the plan counts practice hours rather than interviews.

## What one interview costs

A 30-minute interview costs about $0.42 (31p), and scoring the answers is half of it. Since 26 Oct, caching the job and CV between answers and scoring follow-ups in the same call bring it to about $0.37 (27p). The tables below still use 31p, so every margin is a little better than shown. Figures are for 8 questions, about 17 minutes of you speaking and 6 follow-ups.

## Cost per user per month

Most users will cost between £1 and £4 a month; a very active job seeker costs about £11.

| User | Jobs set up | Interviews | Drill questions | Presentations | AI cost a month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light: one interview coming up | 1 | 3 | 10 | None | $1.66 (£1.24) |
| Typical: 2 to 3 weeks of prep | 2 | 8 | 30 | 2 runs, 1 Q&A | $4.79 (£3.59) |
| Heavy: active job search | 5 | 25 | 80 | 6 runs, 3 Q&A | $14.37 (£10.77) |

The worst case is someone who speaks for the full 120-minute daily limit every day. That is about 210 interviews and $88 (£66) in a month, more than any of the prices below. The daily limit covers the whole app today; once accounts exist, each plan needs its own monthly limit. A cap of 10 practice hours a month holds one person to about £6.25.

Fixed costs come on top: hosting, database, file storage, email and error alerts, roughly £50 to £100 a month at the start. That is covered by about 5 paying users.

## Launch prices, then full prices

Lead with the 3-month Job search plan: the average UK job search takes about 3.8 months, so it matches how people look for work. Monthly covers a short burst before one interview. Both are capped in practice hours rather than interviews, because interviews run from 10 to 60 minutes and cost follows time. The 3-month hours are one pool, not 10 a month, because interviews come in bursts. The 14-day Interview Pass was dropped on 26 Oct. Prices include 20% VAT; the figures are for the launch price.

| Plan | Launch price | Later | Includes | You keep after VAT and card fees | Typical AI cost | Left over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | £0 | £0 | One 10-minute interview for one job, browser voice, no card | £0 | £0.17 | −£0.17 |
| Monthly | £14.99 a month | £29 | 10 hours of practice a month (about 20 interviews of 30 minutes), presentations, story bank | £11.96 | £4.47 | £7.50 (63%) |
| Job search (highlighted) | £34.99 for 3 months | £69 | 30 hours to use any time in 3 months, everything included, paid once and never renews | £28.43 | £10.08 over 3 months | £18.35 (65%); £9.68 (34%) if all 30 hours are used |

**Extra hours:** 5 hours for £7.99 on any paid plan, used after the plan's own hours. That is £1.60 an hour, a little more than Monthly (£1.50) or Job search (£1.17), so the plans stay the better deal. If all 5 hours are used, it still leaves £3.21 of the £6.34 you keep after VAT and card fees.

Lower prices need a firm limit, and hours are fairer than interviews: a 60-minute interview costs six times as much as a 10-minute one. At the 10-hour cap a Monthly user costs about £6.25 and still leaves £5.71, so the plan never loses money. A Job search user who uses all 30 hours still leaves £9.68. The free interview has to be cheap too: if 1 in 20 free users pays, 10 minutes with the browser voice costs about £3.30 per paying user, against £7.20 for a full 20-minute one. When prices go up, let early users keep their launch price; it rewards them and gives people a reason to buy now.

At launch, Hakkiri costs about the same as the cheapest delivery coach (Yoodli Pro, $11 a month) while building each interview from your job and CV, which Yoodli doesn't. The later prices sit between Yoodli and the full prep courses (Big Interview, $39 for 30 days), and far below a human mock interview (about $179 a session).

In the mobile app, Apple and Google take 15% of in-app purchases on their small-business rates, worked out on the price before VAT. On £14.99 a month that is about £1.87, so either sell through the web or accept the smaller margin.

## How to keep costs down

The biggest saving is a monthly limit per person; the rest trim 5% to 20% each.

Fixed on 26 Oct while working this out:

- **A launch blocker.** Hakkiri sent a temperature setting that Claude Sonnet 5 rejects, so every scoring and question-writing call would have failed with real keys. It is no longer sent.
- **Thinking turned off.** Sonnet 5 thinks before answering by default, billed as extra output and slower. Hakkiri's calls are short tasks checked in code, so it is now off.
- **Cheaper fast model.** Follow-up decisions moved from Haiku 4.5 ($1/$5 per million tokens, now a legacy model) to Haiku 5.5 ($0.10/$0.50).

## Assumptions and sources

These are estimates until the real-key test: Settings > Usage will show the actual cost of each interview, and this doc should be checked against it.

- **Prompt sizes** were measured from Hakkiri's own prompts with a typical CV (6,500 characters), job spec (4,500), cover letter (2,500) and company page (6,000), at about 2.5 characters per token. Reply sizes are estimates, and 15% of main-model calls are assumed to be asked twice after failing a check.
- **A 30-minute interview** is 8 questions, about 17 minutes of the candidate speaking, 6 follow-ups and 1,700 characters of interviewer voice.
- **Prices:** Claude Sonnet 5 $2 in / $10 out and Haiku 5.5 $0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens ([Claude pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing), [Haiku 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/haiku-5-5/overview)). Deepgram Nova-3 batch $0.0043 a minute ([Activepieces, Oct 2026](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/deepgram-pricing-2026-per-minute-rates-and-add-ons)). ElevenLabs Flash $0.05 per 1,000 characters on the Creator plan ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/weve-lowered-api-agents-pricing-and-introduced-pay-as-you-go)).
- **Model settings:** Sonnet 5 rejects temperature and thinks by default ([Sonnet 5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/sonnet-5/overview), [thinking](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/thinking)).
- **Money:** £1 = $1.3342 on 26 Oct 2026 ([Pound Sterling Live](https://www.poundsterlinglive.com/data/currencies/gbp-pairs/GBPUSD-exchange-rate)). Card fees 1.5% + 20p for UK cards, plus 0.7% for Stripe Billing on subscriptions ([We Are Founders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/stripe-fees-uk-2026/)). 20% UK VAT included in every price.
- **Open question:** whether Deepgram charges more for recordings opted out of its model-improvement programme (Hakkiri opts out). Check before launch.
