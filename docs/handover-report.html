Delivered Desktop Modules & Operational Status
1. Authentication & Security Live & Operational
Production-grade cryptographic account security and session management.
  • PBKDF2 + SHA-512 password hashing with unique 16-byte random salts.
  • Persistent disk storage (survives system restarts and crashes).
  • Full REST auth endpoints: signup, login, session validation, and logout.
  • Dynamic user profile binding across desktop header and navigation rail.
  • Integrated password reset with token generation.
2. 7-Step Diagnostic Assessment Completed
Calibrated intake onboarding establishing an executive speaking baseline.
  • Evaluates seniority, industry track, and primary communication friction points.
  • Automated generation of tailored 4-week speech mastery plans.
  • Interactive visual illustrations and progress tracking.
  • Gated dashboard access ensuring first-time user calibration.
3. Communication Agility Gym Live & Operational
Spontaneous executive speaking drills under real-time constraints.
  • 6 drill modes: Think First (BLUF), PREP, Filler Eliminator, Brevity, Metric Impact, Tone.
  • Live microphone recording via Web Audio API & MediaRecorder.
  • Interactive audio waveform visualization and instant playback booth.
  • Acoustic heuristic scoring with targeted improvement suggestions.
4. 4-Pillar Speech Analytics Completed
Objective quantitative clarity profiling with radar telemetry.
  • Measures Clarity (94% benchmark), Structure, Brevity, and Impact.
  • Radar chart visualization with trend historical metrics.
  • Conversational filler word tracking (1.2 fillers/min target).
  • Streak tracking (XP and practice continuity calendar).
5. AI Interview Simulation Lab Live & Operational
Full-scale role-specific rehearsal with adaptive AI interviewer.
  • Company, role, and CV parsing for tailored question generation.
  • Live AI interviewer voice (Sam) powered by ElevenLabs & Web Speech API.
  • Adaptive probing follow-up questions responding to candidate answers.
  • Readiness score reports, pacing indicators, and CV-aligned rewrites.
6. Presentation & Pitch Lab Completed
Slide-by-slide rehearsal with overrun detection and Q&A practice.
  • Supports slide deck imports (PDF, PowerPoint, and structured text).
  • Time budget tracking per slide with visual timeline overrun alerts.
  • Simulated executive audience Q&A across 4 persona styles.
  • Readiness scoring evaluating pitch opening, message, and ask.
7. STAR Story Bank Vault Completed
Structured leadership accomplishment repository.
  • Situation, Task, Action, and Result (STAR) framework alignment.
  • Automated question-fit mapping for competency prompts.
  • Printable 1-page morning executive prep sheet.
8. Studio Settings & Calibration Completed
Hardware calibration, telemetry preferences, and privacy controls.
  • Live microphone calibration and ambient noise check.
  • Zero accent bias guarantee (scores logic and cadence only).
  • GDPR-compliant "Export All My Data" (ZIP format) and full data wipe.
